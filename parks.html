National Parks of Kazakhstan

Kazakhstan has many beautiful national parks. They protect animals, plants and natural landscapes.

Famous National Parks

Charyn National Park

Charyn National Park is famous for the Charyn Canyon and its beautiful natural landscapes.

The park is located in the southeast of Kazakhstan.

Learn More

Altyn-Emel National Park

Altyn-Emel is one of the largest national parks in Kazakhstan.

It is known for its desert landscapes, mountains and the Singing Dune.

Learn More

Natural Areas

Mountains

Mountain areas provide habitats for many plants and animals.

Deserts

Desert landscapes are home to specially adapted animals and plants.

Steppes

The steppe is one of the main natural regions of Kazakhstan.

Why Protect National Parks?

  1. National parks protect natural habitats.
  2. They help protect endangered animals.
  3. They preserve beautiful landscapes.
  4. They are important for education and tourism.
